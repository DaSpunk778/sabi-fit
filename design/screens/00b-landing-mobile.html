How it works

Three steps.
No fluff.

01

Pick your day

Chest, biceps, arms, shoulders, back or legs. Your last session for that day loads by itself, ready to go.

02

Log every set

Reps for push-ups, kg × reps for dumbbells. Tick a set and the rest timer starts on its own.

03

Beat last time

Every set shows what you did last time. New bests light up and your charts keep the score.

SABI FIT is also my classroom. Every screen here taught me something new about building real products.
